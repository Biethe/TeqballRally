Google Play Data Safety Requirement

Account & Data Deletion Request

In compliance with Google Play's User Data policy and international data protection laws (GDPR/CCPA), players of Teqball Rally can request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated gameplay data.

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can delete your account and wipe all stored data immediately from inside the game without submitting a request:

  1. Open Teqball Rally on your device.
  2. Tap SETTINGS on the title screen.
  3. Select Legal & Privacy.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Data and confirm your choice.

This immediately removes your account from the leaderboards, disbands or leaves your clubs, and deletes your local save file.

What Data Is Deleted?

Upon processing your deletion request:

  • Player Identity & Profile: Nickname, unique player token, and account record are permanently purged from the server database.
  • Leaderboard Entries: Your scores, trophies, and rank are permanently removed from all global and regional leaderboards.
  • Club Memberships: You are removed from all clubs (if you are the owner, ownership is transferred or the club is disbanded).
  • Friend Links: All mutual friend connections are deleted.

Data Retention Timeline

In-app deletions are executed instantaneously. Web requests sent by email are processed and confirmed within 48 hours.
