# Privacy Policy for Teqball Rally

**Effective Date:** September 17, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 17, 2026

Welcome to **Teqball Rally** ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience playing our game. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you play our mobile game application.

---

## Key Highlights
- **No Selling of Data:** We do not sell your personal data to any third party.
- **Offline & Guest Friendly:** The game can be played fully offline without registration.
- **Mandatory Account Deletion:** You can delete your account and all associated server data at any time from within the game (`Settings -> Legal & Privacy -> Delete Account & Data`) or via our public Data Deletion Request Page (`public/data-deletion.html`).
- **Encrypted in Transit:** All communications with our servers are encrypted using modern HTTPS/WSS (TLS 1.3).

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide to Us
- **Player Nickname:** If you choose to sign up for online leaderboards, clubs, or matchmaking, you create a public nickname. No real name or email address is required to play.
- **Club Information:** Club names and membership rosters you create or join.

### B. Automatically Collected Information
- **Game Progress & Stats:** Single-player progression, character levels, unlocked venues, match scores, trophies, and tournament achievements (stored locally on your device and synchronized to the game server if signed in).
- **Device & Network Diagnostics:** Anonymous crash reports, device model, operating system version, and latency metrics to diagnose connectivity issues and prevent application crashes.

### C. In-App Purchase Information
- In-app purchases (e.g., The Arena lifetime unlock and coin packs) are processed directly by the Google Play Store billing service. We do not receive, store, or process your credit card or payment credentials. Transaction validation is handled securely via **RevenueCat**.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information exclusively to:
- Provide, operate, and maintain game features (e.g., career mode, local matches, and real-time online 1v1 matchmaking).
- Display public leaderboards and friend lists.
- Validate in-app purchases and restore purchased entitlements across sessions.
- Send optional game notifications (e.g., stamina recovery, match challenges, or weekend cup events).
- Detect and fix technical bugs, ANRs, and crashes to improve gameplay stability.

---

## 3. Third-Party Service Providers
We may share minimal data with trusted third-party infrastructure providers solely to operate the Game:
- **Google Play Services:** Core application distribution, Android runtime services, and Play Store billing.
- **RevenueCat:** Receipt verification and in-app purchase entitlement management.
- **OneSignal:** Optional push notification delivery for game events (users can opt out in device settings).
- **Sentry:** Crash and performance diagnostic monitoring.

---

## 4. Data Retention & Account Deletion
We retain your gameplay data only for as long as your account is active or needed to provide you with online services.

**How to Delete Your Data:**
- **In-App:** Navigate to `Settings -> Legal & Privacy -> Delete Account & Data` to immediately wipe your account, remove club memberships, and clear all local data.
- **Web:** You can submit a deletion request via our public Data Deletion Request Page (`public/data-deletion.html`) using your 8-character player code.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
Teqball Rally is designed as a sports game for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information (such as real names, physical addresses, or phone numbers) from children under the age of 13.
